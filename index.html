How we work

The daily and weekly rhythms of the team.

Daily

Start of day

Check Teams and email for anything urgent. Identify the one or two things that move the needle today.

End of day

Share what moved, what is blocked, and what is next. Update your task status. If something is stuck, flag it; do not wait for the next meeting.

Weekly

Monday

Weekly stand-up with Bryton. Set the week's priorities. Review the Helix RAID log and flag anything that needs attention this week.

Thursday

Helix demo session. Accept or reject features against acceptance criteria. Surface anything that changes the parity list or timeline.

Friday

End-of-week check: what shipped, what slipped, what carries into next week. Update the GitHub project board so Monday's stand-up starts clean.

No client PII in Claude

Do not enter client names, account numbers, SINs, dates of birth, or any personally identifiable information into Claude. Anonymise and describe, do not paste. This applies to all AI tools.

Working style

Working with Bryton

A summary of how Bryton works. Read the full "working with me" document in Notion for more detail.

Lead with the recommendation, then the reasoning. Be direct; no padding.
Say "I don't know" when unsure. Flag uncertainty. Separate fact from inference.
Ask when critical context is missing. Correct errors immediately.
Favour simple over complex. Daily or weekly stand-ups over heavy project management tooling.
Build outputs any teammate can reuse. No personal shortcuts, undocumented assumptions, or one-off formats.
Verify before sending. Financial figures independently checked. External claims cited.
